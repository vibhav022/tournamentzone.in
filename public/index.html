No engines, outside assistance or shared accounts. Follow Chess.com’s fair-play rules. This is an independently organised tournament.

A few things before you join.

Do I need a Chess.com account?

Yes. Create a free account on Chess.com and enter your exact username when registering. The games will be conducted there.

What happens if the tournament is cancelled?

If the tournament does not take place, your full paid registration amount will be refunded to the original payment method. See the refund policy for the process and timings.

My college is not in the list. Can I register?

Yes. Type the full name of your Indore college and select “Use this college name”. The organiser may verify your student status.

My payment was deducted but I see “pending”.

Use “Check registration” with your reference and access code. We check the payment with the provider before confirming your place. If it remains pending, contact the organiser with the reference; do not pay again.
