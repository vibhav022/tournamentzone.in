Cancellation & refund policy

If the tournament does not happen, you get your entry fee back.

If the tournament is cancelled or does not take place, every participant who has paid is entitled to a full refund of the registration amount paid.

If the organiser cancels the event

The organiser will inform registered participants using their contact details and initiate a full refund of the paid entry fee through the payment provider. Refunds go to the original payment method. No cancellation deduction will be made from the registration amount.

Refund timing

The organiser aims to initiate cancellation refunds within seven working days after cancellation is confirmed. Once a refund is initiated, the payment provider and your bank control when it appears in your account. Processing time varies by payment method; contact the organiser if the provider’s expected timeline has passed.

If the date changes

If the event is rescheduled and you cannot attend the new time, contact the organiser before the rescheduled event begins to request a full refund. The new date and instructions will be shared through your registered contact details.

Duplicate, failed or uncertain payments

If you are charged more than once for the same entry, contact the organiser with the registration reference and payment IDs for reconciliation and a refund of the duplicate amount. If money was deducted but the registration is pending, first use Check registration. Do not pay again until the earlier payment is checked.

Participant withdrawal and fair play

Contact the organiser before the tournament begins if you need to withdraw or correct an entry. Individual withdrawal requests are reviewed case by case. Missing a scheduled match or disqualification for confirmed cheating does not automatically qualify for a refund. This does not affect the full-refund promise if the tournament does not take place.

How to request help

The organiser’s support contact will be published before paid registration opens. Keep your registration reference and payment ID for support.

Include your reference number, payment ID and a short description of the problem. Never send your card details, UPI PIN, admin password or private registration access code. The organiser does not need these to issue a refund.
