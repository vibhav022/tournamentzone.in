Privacy policy

Your information, handled with care.

This policy applies to information submitted to the organiser through Tournament Zone for the Indore college chess tournament.

What we collect

We collect your name, email address, mobile number, college, year of study and Chess.com username. We also store your registration reference, consent version and date, payment amount, payment status, and provider order, payment and refund identifiers. Security controls temporarily store hashed network identifiers and login attempt counts.

Why we collect it

We use this information to process registration and payment, check eligibility, contact you about the schedule and joining instructions, resolve payment issues, prevent misuse and arrange refunds. Registration does not subscribe you to marketing.

Payments and the tournament platform

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We do not store your full card number, CVV, banking password or UPI PIN. Never provide these details to the organiser. Games take place on Chess.com, which processes your account and game activity under its own policies. This website is independently organised and is not endorsed by Chess.com.

Who can access the information

Authorised tournament organisers can access registration records. Hosting, database and payment providers process information needed to operate the service: Vercel, Neon and Razorpay. Chess.com handles gameplay. We do not sell your personal information or publish your phone number or email address.

Storage and retention

Registration information remains in the organiser’s database until it is removed. The organiser should review and remove personal information no longer needed after the event and after support and refund requests are resolved. Payment records may need to be retained for accounting or legal requirements. Removing an entry from the active player list can preserve a restricted financial record so that payments and refunds can still be reconciled.

Cookies and device storage

An essential, HTTP-only session cookie keeps administrators signed in for up to eight hours. Your browser may store your registration reference and private access code so you can return to your entry. Clear your browser’s site data to remove this local copy. No advertising or analytics cookies are included by this website. Razorpay and Chess.com may use their own storage when you use their services.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of your registration information by contacting the organiser. Verification may be required to prevent someone else accessing your data. Some payment records may be retained where required to complete a refund, settle a dispute or meet record-keeping obligations.

Student eligibility and safety

Provide accurate information and keep your access code private. If you are under 18, obtain permission from a parent or guardian before submitting personal details or making a payment. Do not upload identity documents to this form. If eligibility checks are needed, ask the organiser for a secure method.

Contact and changes

The organiser’s support contact will be published before paid registration opens. Contact the organiser for registration, privacy or refund questions.

Material changes to this policy will be reflected here. Check this page before submitting a new registration.
