Tournament terms

A fair tournament starts with clear rules.

Eligibility and registration

This tournament is for students of colleges in Indore. Enter your real name, current college, year of study and your own Chess.com username. One active registration is allowed per email address and Chess.com username. The organiser may verify college enrolment. If you are under 18, obtain permission from a parent or guardian before registering or paying.

Entry fee and confirmation

The current entry fee is shown on the registration form before checkout. Creating a reference number does not confirm your place: your payment must be captured and verified. Save the receipt, reference and private access code. Any later fee change applies only to newly created registrations.

Schedule and playing platform

The tournament will be conducted on Chess.com. The date, time, format, rounds and time control will be published on the tournament page or shared by the organiser before play. When the date is marked “To be announced”, you are registering before a date has been fixed. Keep your contact details accurate and check joining instructions.

Prizes

Winner: ₹500. Second place: ₹250. Third place: ₹100. Final placements follow the announced tournament format and tie-break rules. The organiser will verify results and contact winners to arrange prize payments. Prizes are not guaranteed to every participant and are separate from cancellation refunds.

Fair play

Use only your own account. Chess engines, bots, outside help, account sharing and collusion are prohibited. Follow Chess.com’s platform and fair-play policies. The organiser may remove incorrect or fraudulent entries and review suspected misconduct. Contact the organiser to dispute an entry correction or tournament decision.

Connection and attendance

You are responsible for a working device, internet connection and access to your Chess.com account. Join at the announced time. Chess.com’s rules and the organiser’s announced format govern disconnections, missed games and tie-breaks.

Cancellation and refunds

If the tournament does not happen, the full registration amount paid will be refunded to the original payment method. See the cancellation and refund policy for details about rescheduling and support.

Independent organisation

Tournament Zone is a registration and organiser service. Games take place on Chess.com. The event is not operated, sponsored or endorsed by Chess.com unless the organiser separately provides evidence of such an arrangement.

Contact

The organiser’s support contact will be published before paid registration opens.
